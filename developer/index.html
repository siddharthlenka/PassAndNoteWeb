ABOUT ME

Building around privacy and control.

I’m an iOS developer with an interest in understanding how systems work, from application interfaces and data flows to encrypted storage and recovery.

I created Pass & Note to give passwords and private notes a simple home on your own device, with manual encrypted backups that remain under your control.

Engineering interests

  • Swift and SwiftUI application development
  • Encrypted local storage with GRDB and SQLCipher
  • Key management and platform authentication
  • Portable encrypted backups and local browser recovery
  • End-to-end feature design and practical debugging

INDEPENDENT PROJECT

Pass & Note

A local-first password and secure-notes vault for iOS and Android, with a companion browser recovery viewer. No account is required, and backups are created and stored by the user.

Mobile vaults

Encrypted on-device storage, a Master Password, optional biometric quick unlock, and manual backup and restore.

Recovery viewer

A read-only way to open supported encrypted exports locally in a compatible browser when the mobile app is unavailable.
