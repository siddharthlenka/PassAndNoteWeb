HOW IT WORKS

Your vault, from setup to recovery.

Five simple steps. Your device remains the home of your vault.

01

Create your vault

Choose a strong Master Password. The app generates a random vault key and creates your encrypted local database.

  1. Create Master Password
  2. Generate random vault key
  3. Create encrypted database
02

Add your information

Add passwords and secure notes to your local vault. Organize and edit them in the mobile app.

  1. Passwords
  2. Secure notes
  3. One local vault
03

Lock your vault

Use Lock when you finish. Your selected auto-lock interval also controls authentication when returning from the background.

  1. Lock
  2. Database closes
  3. Sensitive content hidden
  4. Authentication required
04

Create an encrypted backup

In Vault, choose Export Vault and confirm your Master Password. Save the encrypted file somewhere you can access if your phone is unavailable.

  1. Vault
  2. Encrypted export
  3. .pnvault
  4. Your chosen storage
05

Recover when needed

Choose your encrypted export in the Recovery Viewer and enter the Master Password used to create it. Read the recovered information locally.

  1. .pnvault
  2. Recovery Viewer
  3. Local decryption
  4. Read-only recovery
